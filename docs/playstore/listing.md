## Title

AtPlace: Location Reminders

## Short Description

Get reminded automatically the moment you arrive at a place you've saved.

## Full Description

AtPlace is a privacy-focused reminder app that notices where you are, so you don't have to remember to check a list. Save the places you visit often—Home, Work, Gym, Supermarket, Pharmacy, or anywhere else—and attach reminders to them. The moment you arrive (or leave), AtPlace shows you the reminder automatically, right when it's actually useful.

WHY ATPLACE

A to-do list only helps if you remember to open it. A time-based reminder only helps if you happen to be in the right place when it goes off. AtPlace ties the reminder to the place itself, so it shows up exactly when you can act on it—no manual checking, no guessing when to leave home.

KEY FEATURES

• Location-triggered reminders — Powered by geofencing, reminders fire automatically when you arrive at, or leave, a saved place.
• Saved places — Keep your frequently visited spots (Home, Work, Gym, Supermarket, and more), added by saving your current location, searching an address, or picking a point on the map.
• Configurable trigger radius — Choose how close counts as "arrived" for each place.
• Smart delay options — Set a per-reminder notification delay (immediately, or 1–10 minutes) so briefly passing by a place doesn't trigger a false alert.
• Once or repeating reminders — A reminder can fire once and turn itself off, or keep firing every time you return.
• Map view — See every saved place laid out on one map.
• Backup & restore — Export your places and reminders to a file whenever you like, and restore them on the same or a new device.
• Dark mode — A full dark theme that follows your system setting or your own preference.
• Event log — Optional diagnostics (Developer Mode) showing exactly when and how each reminder was delivered.

PRIVACY FIRST

AtPlace works entirely on your device. There's no account to create, no cloud sync, and your location is never sent anywhere—it's used only to power the reminders you've set up for yourself.

Download AtPlace and let your next reminder find you, right where it matters.

## Feature Highlights
